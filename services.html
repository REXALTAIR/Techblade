Most repairs are completed within 24–48 hours. Simple fixes like software installation can be done same-day. Complex hardware repairs may take 3–5 days depending on parts availability.

Yes! All our repairs come with a 30-day service warranty. If the same issue recurs within 30 days, we'll fix it for free. Parts replacement carries the manufacturer's warranty.
